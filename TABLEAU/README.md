# Learn Tableau in Under 2 Hours: Dashboards & Relationships

This folder documents my introduction to Tableau, exploring its unique approach to data visualization and relationship management. This project focused on adapting to a new UI and handling real-world data discrepancies.

## WHAT I LEARNT
* **UI Walkthrough:** Navigating the Tableau workspace and understanding the "Shelf" and "Marks" card logic.
* **Data Connectivity:** Implementing Joins and Relationships to structure Kaggle datasets for analysis.
* **Data Organization:** Creating Bins and calculated fields to categorize data effectively.
* **Interactive Dashboards:** Building a Mini Project that combines multiple visualizations into a single, cohesive story.

## OBSERVATIONS & DISCREPANCIES
* **Year-Based Data Shifts:** Because the tutorial used a 2024 dataset and the Kaggle source had been updated, differences in the data were inevitable. I focused on the tool's mechanics rather than matching the tutorial's numbers exactly.
* **Visual Complexity:** Observed that Tableau offers a vast variety of ways to show the same table, which initially presented a challenge in choosing the most effective visualization.

## CHALLENGES
* **UI Confusion:** Adapting to the way Tableau displays and handles tables was significantly different from Excel or Power BI, requiring a shift in perspective.
* **Software Gaps:** Encountered features that did not behave as easily as shown in the tutorial, likely due to version updates or interface changes.

## SOLUTIONS
* **Growth Mindset:** Decided to move forward through discrepancies to maintain momentum, prioritizing the "learning of a new tool" over perfect replication.
* **AI-Assisted Navigation:** Used AI to research specific workarounds and UI adjustments when the tutorial's path was no longer available or functioning differently in the current version.
